# Porter

Based on the recipe at [brewersfriend.com](FIXME)

## Ingredients

* 60 g munich malt
* 100 g torrified wheat
* 2.04 kg extra pale malt
* 150 g oats
* 175 g crystal light malt
* 120 g chocolate malt
* 75 g dark malt

* 20 g fuggle hops (boil 60 min)
* 20 g fuggle hops (boil 30 min)
* 1/2 tsp (~2g) irish moss

* Crossmyloofbrew 'Ale' yeast (1/2 packet)
* 8.5 litres water (mash)
* 4.5 litres water (sparge)
* 3 litres water (before yeast)
