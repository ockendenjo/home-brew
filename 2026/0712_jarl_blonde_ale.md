# Jarl Blonde Ale

Based on the recipe at [brewersfriend.com](https://www.brewersfriend.com/homebrew/recipe/view/1631845/fyne-ales-jarl-session-blonde-ale)

## Ingredients

* 0.55 kg maris otter malt
* 1.05 kg extra pale malt
* 35 g torrified wheat

* 10 g citra hops (boil 60 min)
* 10 g citra hops (boil 45 min)
* 10 g citra hops (boil 30 min)
* 10 g citra hops (boil 15 min)
* 10 g citra hops (dry hop)
* 1/2 tsp (~2g) irish moss

* Crossmyloofbrew 'Ale' yeast (1/2 packet)
* 8.5 litre water (mash)
* 4 litre water (sparge)
* 0.5 litre water (added during boil)
